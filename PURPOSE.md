A personal AI-output-humanizer skill that removes AI writing patterns from prose. Synthesized from the best patterns in conorbronsdon/avoid-ai-writing, blader/humanizer, brandonwise/humanizer, stephenturner/skill-deslop, and lguz/humanize-writing-skill. Three modes (rewrite, detect, edit), voice calibration from sample or named profiles, and iterate-to-convergence with self-audit. Detect mode treats AI-soundingness as an aesthetics problem, a measure of style tells, never a claim about who or what wrote the text.
